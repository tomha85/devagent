## Permitted descriptive use

You may make truthful, nominative references such as:

- "compatible with DevAgent";
- "forked from DevAgent";
- "uses the DevAgent open-source core";
- "based on the DevAgent project".

Such references must not create confusion about whether your product, service, fork, report, or qualification is official or endorsed by the DevAgent project.

## Forks and modified distributions

If you redistribute a materially modified fork, use branding that clearly distinguishes it from the official DevAgent project. Do not present a modified fork as an official DevAgent release, certified DevAgent distribution, or official DevAgent commercial offering without separate written permission.

## Certification and commercial claims

Do not use phrases such as "DevAgent Certified", "Official DevAgent", or equivalent claims for third-party products, qualification results, services, or distributions unless the project owner has explicitly authorized that use.

## Logos and brand assets

Do not use DevAgent logos as the primary branding for an unrelated product or service, and do not modify or combine project branding in a way that implies official endorsement.

## Questions

For trademark or branding permission beyond the uses described here, contact the DevAgent project owner through the official repository owner account.

This policy is intended to prevent source-code licensing from being confused with rights to project identity and endorsement.
